My philosophy

Good software respects the person on the other side of the glass. It opens quickly, explains itself without a manual, and stays steady on imperfect networks and modest hardware.

Working as both designer and engineer lets me hold the whole arc in one pair of hands. Typography, layout rhythm, state machines, and bundle weight get decided together instead of lost in handoffs.
