I build

I build software that does real work and ships to real people. I make AI assistants that run entirely on a phone (no cloud required), systems that answer questions by reading actual documents instead of guessing, and the apps, servers and automations behind them. In plain terms: I teach machines to do useful tasks, then make the whole thing fast, reliable and pleasant to use.

I care just as much about how it looks and feels as how it runs. The spacing, the typography, the animations, the small details that make an app feel made by a person and not stamped out by a template. I have founded and launched products people actually use, and I work across Rust, C/C++, Python and TypeScript, from low-level native code up to the interface.
