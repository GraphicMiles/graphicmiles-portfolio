The listing file before the money.

Every home arrives with information, complete or not. These four parts decide how clear the rental conversation can be.

Owner and agent identity

Who is listing the home, who owns it and what authority the agent has to represent the property.

Inspection proof

Photos, checklist and property details captured during an assigned inspection.

Availability and terms

When the home is available, what the landlord accepts and which terms must be agreed before signing.

Total move-in cost

Rent, service fees, deposit and schedule shown before any payment is requested.

What we will not promise.

Trust starts with the limits. These are ours, in plain language.

What the desk will do

  • Show which listing checks are complete, and what they do not cover.
  • Name who is responsible for the property, terms and next step.
  • Keep the listing, schedule and next steps visible.
  • Flag missing information before it becomes a payment surprise.

What the desk will not do

  • Guarantee approval, availability or a landlord decision.
  • Hide fees or pressure you toward a payment schedule.
  • Replace independent legal, financial or property advice.
  • Hold customer money in an ilee wallet or promise a payout structure before it is approved.

What is ilee building?

ilee is building a trust-first property service for Lagos: verified homes to rent or buy, clear schedules and human support from viewing to handover.

What is live in Phase 1?

The live pilot covers web discovery, enquiries, staff-led listing checks, assisted applications and reference-based status lookup in Lekki and Ikeja. Homes for sale can be browsed and enquired about, and the desk checks the title before one is published. What is not live is completing a purchase or a payment: accounts, KYC, automated affordability, payment mandates, wallets, payouts, short-lets, commercial leasing and mobile apps are all still ahead of us.

How do I see homes and speak with the desk?

Open Homes to see published pilot homes. If none are live, use the web form with your area, budget and timing. When a home fits, the desk confirms availability and arranges a viewing; there is no live chat or public agent directory in this phase.

Can landlords and agents list?

Yes. List your home takes the details in one form — no account, no login. An agent or caretaker submits with the owner's authority, and a person at the desk confirms that authority and inspects the home before it is visible to anyone.

What is Pay Small Small?

Pay Small Small is what ilee calls renting in agreed installments. Where a landlord accepts a schedule, the desk records that agreement and explains the proposed schedule and the total cost before you commit. This Phase 1 build does not collect rent or activate live payments; it is not an in-house ilee wallet.

Does ilee hold rent or deposits?

No. ilee will not present an in-house wallet or hold customer money directly. Any live payment flow will use a licensed provider and a verified landlord account, subject to final review.

Which areas come first?

The first supply focus is Lekki and Ikeja, to rent or to buy. Tell the desk about another Lagos area through the enquiry form so we can record demand.

How do I join the pilot?

Send an enquiry with your role, area and budget. The desk will contact you when a supported home or supply conversation has a clear next step.

What should I include in an enquiry?

Tenants can include an area, annual rent range and timing. Landlords and agents can include the area, home type, availability and authority to list.
