For owners and their agents

Offer a home to ilee.

Tell us about a home in Lekki or Ikeja. A person at the desk confirms you have the authority to offer it and inspects it in person before any tenant or buyer sees it.

There is no account to create and nothing to log into. You submit once, we take it from there, and a named person calls you back.

What happens after you submit.

Four steps, and only the last one is visible to the public. Nothing you send is published by this form.

  1. 1

    Submitted

    Your details reach the desk as a listing in the queue. It is not on the homes page, and you cannot put it there yourself.

  2. 2

    Authority confirmed

    A person checks that you can offer this home and who owns it. We do not ask for a BVN, NIN, selfie or bank statement to do it.

  3. 3

    Inspected in person

    An ilee inspector walks the home and photographs it. Those photographs are the only images that can ever appear on the listing.

  4. 4

    Live

    Only now does the home appear to tenants and buyers, with the area shown and the exact address held until a viewing is arranged.

About you

The home

Are you offering it to

Tick this only if you would genuinely take the rent in agreed parts. ilee never holds or moves money, and nothing here obliges you to accept a schedule.

Who owns it

Withdraw at any time from the privacy notice or by replying to our message. We will not ask you for a BVN, NIN, selfie or bank statement — not now, and not to list a home.

What we verify

That the home is real, that you have the authority to offer it, and what it will actually cost. An inspector walks it and photographs it; the desk checks the title where a sale depends on it.

What you are not asked for

No BVN, no NIN, no selfie, no bank statement and no account password. The API refuses those fields even if someone posts them, and there is no column to store them in.

What a submission is not

It is not a listing. Nothing you send appears on the homes page until an inspection has passed, and you cannot publish it yourself. That gate is in the database, not in this page.
