What renting really costs.

Annual rent is the first line, not the total. Budget the rest before you commit.

Updated September 20264 min readLagos rental pilot

Annual rent is not the total

A Lagos rental quote can look simple until fees, deposits and payment timing arrive. The listing should show the total move-in cost, the recurring schedule and which lines are still being confirmed.

The full cost list

Pilot costs vary by home and agreement. Confirm every line in writing before paying. No figures below are a promise of the final price.
LineTypical range
Annual rentThe amount stated by the landlord for the tenancy term
Tenant service feeConfirm before commitment
Caution depositConfirm amount, hold and release terms
Agreement or legal feeConfirm what document or service it covers
Agency or sourcing feeConfirm who charges it and when it is due
Payment scheduleMonthly, quarterly or annual, with dates and method
Utilities and move-in costsConfirm amount, hold and release terms
Refund and cancellation termsRead before signing or paying
Total to move inRent plus agreed fees and deposit before handover

The budget rule

Ask for one written total before you commit. Keep the annual rent, first payment, recurring payment, deposit and every fee in the same view. If the total changes, ask why and get the new schedule in writing.

Ask for every line in writing

Every fee, deposit and payment needs a clear purpose, recipient and receipt. Do not treat an unlisted charge as normal simply because it appears at the point of payment.

Take these with you

  • Budget annual rent plus every agreed fee and deposit before moving in.
  • Pilot fees and eligibility are not final until the listing and agreement are confirmed.
  • Ask who receives each fee, when it is due and what it covers.
  • A clear refund and cancellation rule is cheaper than a payment surprise.
