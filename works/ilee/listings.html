    How a home earns its place.

    A physical inspection is the starting point. Read each home’s badge and ask the desk about its remaining checks.

    1. 1

      Inspect the home

      Condition, access, basic property details and inspection evidence are recorded before the home is published.

    2. 2

      Check owner or agent authority

      The listing records who owns the home or has authority to represent it, with the right documents requested for the pilot.

    3. 3

      Publish the terms

      Availability, accepted schedule, fees, refund terms and the next step are written down before staff creates an application.

    4. 4

      Show the total cost

      Rent, service fees, deposit and proposed payment dates are explained before commitment. This Phase 1 build does not collect payment.
