About ilee

A clearer rental decision, kept in writing.

ilee is building a trust-first property service for Lagos, starting with verified homes to rent or buy in Lekki and Ikeja.

Why ilee exists.

Rent in Lagos is often a lump sum, a long list of fees and a property file that arrives too late. Tenants need a home they can trust. Landlords need serious enquiries and a clearer handover.

Ilee exists so that the rental conversation starts with the listing file, not with suspicion. We show what was checked, what is missing and what happens next.

We are starting with long-term rentals and purchases in Lekki and Ikeja, working with tenants, landlords, agents and caretakers. Every supported home should have a clear listing record, terms and next action.

The pilot is not a bank, lender, insurer or tenancy guarantor. We will not present an in-house wallet or hide the cost of a rental decision.

The people around the home.

Three roles shape the pilot. Each needs a different kind of clarity before the next step.

T

Tenant

The person moving in

Needs an available home, a visible total cost and a schedule that can be understood before the desk creates an application.

L

Landlord

The supply partner

Needs serious tenants, clear terms, property information and a reliable record of what happens next.

A

Agent or caretaker

The local partner

Needs authority to list, a shared viewing process and a fair way to keep owners and tenants aligned.

What Phase 1 includes.

The wider SRS describes buying, short-lets, commercial leasing, KYC, automated affordability, payment mandates, wallets, payouts and mobile apps. Those are future phases, not live services in this Phase 1 rental pilot.

  • Cost before commitment

    The rent, fees, schedule and refund terms are shown before a tenant commits.

  • One listing, one record

    Every home keeps one record of the owner or agent, inspection, availability, terms and status.

  • Human support first

    When a viewing, application or payment question needs attention, the next action and expected response are explained.

  • Flag gaps early

    When a home, schedule or document is not ready, we say so before payment or signing.
