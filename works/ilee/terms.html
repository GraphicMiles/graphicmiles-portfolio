Terms.

What ilee is building, what the pilot does not promise, and how rental enquiries work.

What ilee is

ilee is building a trust-first property service for Lagos. The first focus is long-term homes in Lekki and Ikeja, with verification, visible costs and installment schedules where a landlord accepts them.

What ilee is not

ilee is not a bank, lender, insurer, tenancy guarantor or replacement for independent legal, financial or property advice. A listing, badge or enquiry response does not guarantee availability, approval, affordability or a successful tenancy.

Pilot enquiries

Pilot enquiries begin with the web form or a conversation by email, phone or WhatsApp. The first supply focus is Lekki and Ikeja. We may defer an enquiry where the area, home, authority or information cannot be reasonably checked.

Landlord and agent participation

Participation is by registration and review. Landlords, agents and caretakers must share accurate property information and, where relevant, authority to represent the owner. A pilot listing can be paused or removed if information changes or cannot be checked.

Payments and funds

ilee will not present an in-house wallet or hold customer money directly. Any future payment flow will use a licensed provider and a verified landlord account, subject to provider approval, legal review and the signed terms. Do not send rent or deposits to an ilee account from this site.

Liability and questions

The pilot is a validation service, not an approval or payment instruction. Use the web form or email hello@ilee.ng with questions about the areas or listing process.
