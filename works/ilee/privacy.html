Privacy.

What ilee collects for the Lagos pilot, why, and how to ask for it back.

What we collect

The enquiry form collects what you choose to give: your name, phone number, email, topic and message.

The listing form collects the submitter's name, role, phone and email; the owner's name and phone; an agent name if there is one; and the home's area, street address, type, whether it is offered to rent or to buy, the price, the terms and whether a Pay Small Small schedule is acceptable.

If you go further, the desk may record a viewing arrangement and, where an application is started, the tenancy details needed to vet it. Every form requires a ticked consent box before anything is sent, and each tick is stored as a consent record with the time it was given and the time it was withdrawn, if it ever is.

A street address is used to arrange an inspection and a viewing. It is not published. Tenants and buyers see the area until the desk has confirmed a viewing with them.

What we never collect

We do not ask for a BVN, NIN, selfie, biometric, bank statement or account number — not on a form, not by email, and not to list or rent a home.

This is not only a policy. The API refuses those fields on every public and staff write, including when they are nested inside another field, and the database has no column to put them in. Identity is confirmed by a person at the desk, who records the outcome of that check rather than the identifier it was based on.

Identity-provider integration is deliberately not built in Phase 1. If it is added later, this notice will be updated before it is used, and it will not change what the forms ask you for.

Why we collect it

To reply to your enquiry; to confirm that a home is real and that the person offering it has the authority to do so; to arrange viewings; to vet an application where one is started; and to contact you about the pilot where you have asked us to. We do not sell your details or share them for unrelated marketing.

Our lawful basis is the consent you give when you tick the box, together with the contract of arranging and vetting a home you have asked about. Consent can be withdrawn at any time using the form below, and withdrawing it does not cost you anything.

Your rights under Nigerian law

ilee Nigeria Ltd processes personal data under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, administered by the Nigeria Data Protection Commission, and the General Application and Implementation Directive (GAID) 2025 (NDPC/NDP ACT-GAID/01/2025), issued on 20 March 2025 and in force from 19 September 2025. The GAID replaced the NDPR 2019 as the governing framework.

That gives you the right to know what we hold, to have it corrected, to have it erased, and to withdraw consent. Use the form below or email hello@ilee.ng.

Every request is acknowledged with a reference you can quote. We answer in the same way whether or not we hold data about you, so a response is never a confirmation that you are in our records.

A withdrawal that matches one of your consent records and quotes the reference we gave you is completed immediately, without waiting for a person. Anything else — access, correction, erasure — is handled by the desk, and we will tell you if we need to confirm who you are first.

What erasure actually does here

An approved erasure request redacts your name and removes your contact details, and closes the record. The row itself is kept, because a verification that happened has to remain verifiable: who checked a home, when, and against what.

The audit log is append-only. Nothing in it can be edited or deleted by the application, including by an administrator. Where the law requires us to retain something, we retain it and tell you that we have.

Where your data is processed

The database, the inspection photographs and the hosting of this site are provided by third parties. This site and its API are hosted in Frankfurt, Germany, and the database that holds your details is hosted in London, United Kingdom. Your details are therefore processed outside Nigeria. Where that happens the cross-border transfer rules in the GAID apply, and we rely on the contractual safeguards in our agreements with those providers.

Photographs of a home are held separately from this site and served through a short-lived signed link, so an image of a home that has been unpublished stops being reachable at once.

Keeping your details

We keep pilot responses only as long as needed to run the verification conversations and respond to follow-up, and we keep verification and audit records for as long as the home they relate to is on the platform.

This site

ilee.ng sets no cookies and runs no third-party tracking today. If analytics or consent tools are added later, this notice will be updated before they are used.

Make a request

Withdraw consent, ask what we hold, ask for a correction, or ask for erasure. You will get a reference back.

We may need to check who you are before acting on an access, correction or erasure request.
