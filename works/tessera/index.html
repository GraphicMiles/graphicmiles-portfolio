    Security and control5 controls

    You set the limits. Agents stay inside them.

    Limits live in the contract that holds the funds. An agent cannot talk its way past a cap, because the cap is not part of the conversation.

    • Scoped keys

      Each agent holds its own key, limited to the chains, contracts and vendors you name.

    • Spending caps

      Per-payment and per-day limits are enforced on-chain, not by the agent.

    • Approval gates

      Route anything above a threshold to one or more signers on your team.

    • Instant revoke

      Pause a crew or cancel a key in one click. Pending actions stop before they settle.

    • Permanent audit trail

      Every decision is recorded with the policy version that allowed it.

    Talk to us →
    Does my team need to know anything about crypto?

    No. People sign in with email, SSO or a passkey. Tessera manages keys, network fees and chain selection. Your team sees dollars, vendors and approvals.

    Where do the funds sit?

    In smart accounts that your organisation controls. Agents hold scoped keys that can spend only within the limits in your policy. Tessera cannot move your funds outside those limits.

    What happens if an agent makes a mistake?

    Caps and allowlists limit the damage before it happens. Anything above your threshold waits for a person. You can pause a crew at any time, and each receipt shows which policy allowed the action.

    Which chains do you support?

    Ethereum and the major layer 2 networks, plus Solana. You write a policy once and it applies on every chain you enable.

    Can I use agents I already built?

    Yes. Register them through the SDK and they run under the same policies, approvals and receipts as Tessera's own agents.

    How is pricing set?

    Plans are based on the number of crews and monthly runs. Network fees are passed through at cost. Request access and we will send current pricing.
