If you can send it in a chat, you can send it here

No zipping, no drive links, no scrolling back three months for the file someone asked for.

Memes and images

GIFs, screenshots, photos. They render in the inbox, so the joke lands before the file finishes downloading.

Files and PDFs

Documents up to 10 MB, checked on the server. Private storage, links that expire, no public URLs.

Links

A portfolio, a folder, a playlist. The link arrives with who sent it and when, instead of vanishing into a group chat.

Notes

Just words. A reminder, a thank you, a line you wanted to say properly. Sometimes that is the whole gift.

10 MB per drop

A deliberate limit. Big enough for a document or a long GIF, small enough that a drop arrives in seconds.

Nothing executable

Scripts, HTML uploads and installers are refused, and files are checked by content rather than extension.

Pick. Send. They open it.

No settings to learn, nothing to install, nothing to remember.

01

Create your handle

Sign in with Google and choose a handle. Your email stays private, and people find you by the handle.

02

Send the drop

Enter a handle, attach what you are sending, add a line if you want one. You see the recipient and the item before it goes.

03

They open it

It appears in their inbox right away, and you see when it was opened. Nothing gets lost in a chat thread.

A drop has two readers, that is the whole access list

No public links, no shared folders, no one else reading over a shoulder.

Private by default

Files live in private storage and are served through signed links that expire. There are no guessable public URLs.

Account to account

Only the sender and the recipient can open a drop or download what is inside it.

Block, report, delete

Block a sender, report a drop, turn notifications off, or delete your account and everything in it.

Drop does not hold money

There are no balances, credits, points or top ups. Moving money needs a licensed payment partner and its own compliance work, so it stays separate from digital drops instead of being faked here.
