How long does setup really take?

Most teams collect their first events within eight minutes of signing up. Installing is one script tag and connecting Stripe or your warehouse is a few clicks. Moving from another tool? We migrate your history for you.

Is the data secure and compliant?

Metrix is SOC 2 Type II and GDPR aligned. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, with regional residency, SSO and full audit logs on Enterprise. We never sell or share customer data.

Do I need a card to try it?

No. Every plan starts with a 14-day trial at full features. When it ends you can stay on Starter for free or move up, with no card on file either way.

What happens if I go over my event limit?

We never switch off collection mid-month. We notify you, apply a simple overage rate, and let you move up a plan if the new volume is the new normal.

Can I take my data with me?

Always. Raw events and dashboards export at any time, and the API makes it straightforward to move anywhere else. No exit fee, no lock-in.
