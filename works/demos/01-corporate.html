The problem

Most projects fail between stages, not inside them.

A study hands over to a designer, who hands over to a contractor. Each handover loses a little of the intent, and nobody is left holding the whole.

What we do instead

One team, held to one register.

The engineer who priced the risk sits next to the person who owns it on site. Scope, cost and contingency live in a single register that the client can read at any point.

The result

An asset that is still working in year thirty.

Meridian Light Rail has run its full timetable since the day it opened. Clearwater is producing 340 million litres a day, a third less energy per litre than the plant it replaced.

Ask a question
How do you price risk?

We publish a risk register with the estimate, not after it. Contingency is named, owned and reported monthly, so nobody discovers it at completion. The number we commit to is the number you get, and if the scope changes we say so in writing before we spend anything.

Do you work with our existing consultants?

Frequently. On owner-side commissions we coordinate the whole consultant team, including the ones already appointed. We will say early if a role is duplicated.

What happens after handover?

We stay for the first year of operation on every project, monitoring performance against the design intent and fixing what the models missed. After that the operator can keep us on condition monitoring or take it in house.

Where do you actually operate?

Headquartered in Lagos, with live work across West Africa, East Africa and the Gulf. Nineteen countries so far. We do not bid where we cannot put our own site team.

Can you take a project mid-construction?

Yes, and we will tell you what we find in the first three weeks. Every recovery commission starts with a full audit of the programme, contracts and quality record.
