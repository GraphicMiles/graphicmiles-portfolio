FAQs

What is Bragsheet, and why not just use a notes app?
Notes apps turn into graveyards of unsorted grocery lists and meeting scraps. Bragsheet is purpose-built for career proof: every entry captures what you did, the category, date, and impact so when review season, raise talks, or CV updates arrive, your strongest receipts are already structured.
Can my employer or manager see my Bragsheet?
No. Bragsheet is your personal, private career record, not company HR software. When you switch roles or companies, your entire history of accomplishments stays with you.
Can Claude or ChatGPT read or delete my existing wins?
No. The Bragsheet MCP connector (https://mcp.bragsheet.net/mcp) is deliberately one-way and exposes a single log_win action. Connected AI assistants cannot read your past wins, edit or delete entries, or see your account credentials.
Which AI plans work with Bragsheet AI?
Claude works on every plan, including the free tier. ChatGPT custom MCP connectors require Developer Mode on Plus, Pro, Team, or Enterprise. Mistral Le Chat, Gemini Spark, and standard MCP desktop clients also connect using scoped keys generated in Settings → AI Assistant.
Where can I use Bragsheet right now?
The Bragsheet web app is live right now at app.bragsheet.net across desktop and mobile browsers, and the native Android app is coming soon.
