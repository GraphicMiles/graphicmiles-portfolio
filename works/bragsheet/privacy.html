1. What Information Do We Collect?

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you register on the Services, participate in activities on the Services, or otherwise contact us. The personal information we collect may include:

  • Names, email addresses, usernames, passwords
  • User-submitted content (achievement records, financial log entries)

Important note on financial log entries: These are limited to self-reported information that you manually enter for your own personal tracking purposes (amounts, currency labels, free-text notes). We do not collect, process, or store payment card numbers, bank account details, or transaction history from any payment processor as part of this feature.

Sensitive information: We do not process sensitive information.

Information automatically collected: When you use the Services, we and our analytics provider automatically collect certain usage and device information — including the pages and screens you view, features you use, buttons and links you interact with, general device and browser type, and diagnostic information about errors or crashes. We use this information to understand how the Services are used and to improve them. We also record your interactions within the app (such as taps and on-screen navigation) to reproduce and diagnose issues; entries in password and other input fields are masked and are not captured.

Social Media Login Data: We provide you with the option to register using your existing Google account. If you choose to register this way, we will collect certain profile information from Google, including your name, email address, and profile picture, as described in Section 6 below.

Our use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

2. Optional AI Assistant Connections

You may choose to connect a third-party AI assistant to your Bragsheet account using a personal access key generated in Settings.

When connected and at your instruction, the assistant may submit new achievement entries to your account only. This connection is write-only: the assistant cannot read, view, edit, or delete any existing data in your account.

Content added through an assistant is composed within that third-party service and is subject to that provider’s terms and privacy policy before transmission to Bragsheet.

You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your access key and for all access to or data submissions made through your key.

You may revoke the access key at any time in Settings, which immediately disables the connection.

We may log connection and usage events for security, fraud prevention, and legal compliance purposes.

3. How Do We Process Your Information?

We process your personal information for the following purposes:

  • To facilitate account creation and authentication and otherwise manage user accounts.
  • To deliver and facilitate delivery of services to the user.
  • To process and fulfil in-app purchases. If you purchase the PDF Export Unlock or any other paid feature, your purchase is processed through Google Play Billing. We receive confirmation that a purchase was made so we can grant you access to the purchased feature; we do not receive or store your payment card or bank details ourselves.
  • To respond to user inquiries and offer support to users.
  • To send administrative information to you, including details about our products, services, and changes to our terms and policies.
  • To save or protect an individual’s vital interest, such as to prevent harm.

5. When and With Whom Do We Share Your Personal Information?

We may share your data with the following third parties:

  • Google LLC / Google Cloud Platform (Firebase): Used for user authentication and data storage (Cloud Firestore). Firebase processes data on our behalf as our infrastructure provider and does not use your personal information for its own independent purposes. We maintain a data processing agreement with Google LLC governing this arrangement.
  • Google Play Billing: Used to process the single in-app purchase currently available in the app (a one-time PDF Export Unlock). Google Play Billing handles all payment and card information directly; we never receive, see, or store any payment data ourselves.
  • PostHog, Inc. (product analytics): Used to measure and analyse how the Services are used so we can improve them. We share usage and device information (such as pages viewed, features used, and interaction events), diagnostic error information, session recordings of your interactions within the app, and your account identifier together with your name and email address, so that product usage can be associated with your account. PostHog processes this information as our service provider on our behalf and does not use it for its own independent purposes or for cross-context behavioural advertising.

We may also share your personal information in connection with any merger, sale of company assets, financing, or acquisition of all or a portion of our business.

Our Services offer you the ability to register and log in using your Google account. Where you choose to do this, we will receive certain profile information about you from Google, including your name, email address, and profile picture.

We will use the information we receive only for the purposes described in this Privacy Notice. We do not control, and are not responsible for, other uses of your personal information by Google. We recommend that you review Google’s privacy policy to understand how they collect, use, and share your personal information.

Our servers are located in the United Kingdom. Regardless of your location, your information may be transferred to, stored by, and processed by us in the United Kingdom.

For EU/UK/EEA users: We have implemented Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission for transfers of personal information. Our Standard Contractual Clauses can be provided upon request.

For Nigerian users: The transfer of your personal information to servers located in the United Kingdom is made on the basis of NDPC Standard Contractual Clauses, which provide appropriate safeguards for the protection of your personal data in accordance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. These clauses impose obligations on our infrastructure provider (Google LLC / Firebase) to handle your data to a standard equivalent to what Nigerian law requires. We do not rely solely on your consent to the use of the app as the legal basis for this transfer.

8. How Long Do We Keep Your Information?

We will only keep your personal information for as long as it is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Notice, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

  • Purchase records: retained as required by applicable tax and financial regulations

In the event of a personal data breach likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify the relevant regulatory authority within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach, where required by applicable law.

12. Nigerian Users — Your Rights Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023

This section applies specifically to users located in Nigeria or whose personal information is processed by Bragsheet in connection with Nigeria.

Why Nigerian law applies: Bragsheet is operated from Mowe, Ogun State, Nigeria. The Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) and the regulations made under it apply to our processing of your personal information, regardless of where our servers are located.

Legal bases for processing your data under Nigerian law:

  • Consent: where you have given us clear, affirmative consent. You may withdraw this consent at any time.
  • Contract: where processing is necessary to perform our obligations to you under our Terms and Conditions.
  • Legal obligation: where we are required to process your information to comply with a legal requirement.
  • Legitimate interests: where processing is necessary for our legitimate business interests, provided those interests are not overridden by your rights and interests.

Your rights as a Nigerian data subject under the NDPA include:

  • The right to be informed about how your personal data is being used
  • The right to access your personal data that we hold
  • The right to request correction of inaccurate personal data
  • The right to request deletion or restriction of your personal data
  • The right to object to certain types of processing
  • The right to data portability
  • The right to withdraw consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal

How to exercise your rights: Contact us at legal@bragsheet.net or visit forms.gle/mWE8mx5eKF4XhzVn6. We will respond in accordance with applicable regulatory guidance.

Right to complain to the regulator: If you believe that we have not handled your personal information in accordance with the NDPA, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) at ndpc.gov.ng.

Data breach notification: In the event of a personal data breach that poses a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify the NDPC within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach, in accordance with NDPA obligations.

13. Controls for Do-Not-Track Features

Most web browsers and some mobile operating systems include a Do-Not-Track (‘DNT’) feature or setting. At this stage, no uniform technology standard for recognising and implementing DNT signals has been finalised.
