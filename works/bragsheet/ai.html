How to connect it

You do this once. After that, any win you mention to your assistant can go straight into Bragsheet.

1Step 1 of 3

Add the connector

In your AI assistant's connector settings, add a custom connector using the Bragsheet address below. Leave any API-key or token fields blank, there's no key to paste.

2Step 2 of 3

Approve access

Your assistant opens a Bragsheet screen. Sign in if needed, check the account it says you're authorizing, and tap Allow. That's the whole handshake, nothing to copy.

3Step 3 of 3

Just say it

Tell your assistant “log this as a win” in plain language. It writes the entry to your Bragsheet and confirms.

C Claude
  1. Open Settings, then Connectors and choose Add custom connector (on some plans this is under Customize, then Connectors).
  2. Paste the server address from below. Leave the optional OAuth Client ID, Secret fields blank, Bragsheet sets those up automatically.
  3. Choose Add, then Connect. Bragsheet opens a sign-in and consent screen, confirm the account it shows is yours and tap Allow.
  4. Bragsheet now shows up under Connectors, enable it for a chat via the + button, then just say “add this to my Bragsheet.”

Works on every Claude plan, including Free (Free allows one custom connector). On Team/Enterprise, an admin may need to approve it first.

Set this up on desktop. Adding a custom connector is currently in beta on the Claude mobile app and can be unreliable there. Use claude.ai on a computer, the Claude desktop app, or Chrome on your phone with “Desktop site” turned on. Once it's added, the connector works fine from the mobile app, it's just the initial setup that's best done on desktop.

G ChatGPT

Requires a paid ChatGPT plan. Free ChatGPT accounts cannot add custom connectors at all, this is a ChatGPT platform limit, not a Bragsheet issue. You need Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise, or Edu. (Claude, above, works on any plan, including Free.)

  1. Turn on Developer mode in ChatGPT settings, if you haven't already, it's required for custom connectors that write data, not just read it.
  2. Go to Settings, then Apps & Connectors and choose Create.
  3. Name it “Bragsheet,” paste in the server address below, and finish the sign-in. Bragsheet shows a consent screen, check the account it names is yours, then approve.
  4. Enable Bragsheet from the tools menu in a chat, then say “add this to my Bragsheet.”
M Mistral (Le Chat)
  1. In Bragsheet, go to Settings, then AI Assistant, open Connect your AI, then choose Advanced: assistant can't sign in and click Generate a connection key. Copy it right away, Bragsheet won't show it again.
  2. In Mistral, go to Context, then Connectors, then Add Connector, then switch to the Custom MCP Connector tab.
  3. Fill in a Title (e.g. “bragsheet”) and set Server to the address below, make sure it ends in /mcp.
  4. Under Authentication Method, choose API Token Authentication, leave the header type as Bearer, and paste the connection key you copied in step 1.
  5. Click Connect (or Create). Bragsheet should show as connected under My Connectors.
  6. In a chat, open the + menu, enable Bragsheet under Connectors, then just say “add this to my Bragsheet.”

Mistral's custom connectors currently only support a static token, not OAuth sign-in, so use the connection key above rather than waiting for a sign-in prompt. Treat that key like a password: anyone who has it can add wins to your account.

G Gemini (Spark)
  1. Custom connectors only work inside Gemini Spark, which needs a Google AI Ultra subscription, a personal Google Account (not work/school), being 18+, and having Keep Activity turned on. Spark is not available in every country, this currently includes Nigeria, along with several other countries. Check Google's Gemini Spark availability page to confirm your country before trying to connect.
  2. In Gemini, go to Settings & help, then Connected Apps (on some accounts it's under Personal Intelligence, then Connected Apps first).
  3. Under Custom apps for Spark, click Add a custom app and paste the server address below.
  4. Gemini will try to sign you in through Bragsheet automatically. If it can't, click Show more under Advanced features and paste a bsk_ key instead, generate one in Bragsheet under Settings, then AI Assistant.
  5. Approve the connection, then use it from a Spark task by saying “add this to my Bragsheet.”

This is a Spark-only feature, not standard Gemini chat, if you don't have Google AI Ultra or you're in a country where Spark isn't available yet (Nigeria included), you won't see “Custom apps for Spark” at all, no matter what you try.

Other MCP clients
  1. Find the connector or MCP server settings in your assistant, the exact wording varies, but it's usually under Settings.
  2. Add a new remote MCP server using the address below.
  3. If the client supports OAuth, it'll send you to the Bragsheet sign-in and consent screen, approve it there. If it only takes a static key, open Settings, then AI Assistant in the Bragsheet app, generate a bsk_ key, and paste that instead.
  4. Say “add this to my Bragsheet” at the end of any conversation.

Any assistant that speaks the open MCP standard will work, over OAuth where it's supported, or a bsk_ key where it isn't.

The connector exposes a single action, log_win, it can add a win, and nothing else. Your assistant never receives your Bragsheet password or your account data.

Built to be safe

What it can, and can't, do

The connector is deliberately one-way. It can drop a win in. It can't read, change, or remove anything.

It can

  • Add a new win to your Bragsheet, in your own words
  • Attach a category or tags when you mention them
  • Confirm back to you that the win was saved

It can't

  • Read your existing wins or any of your data
  • Edit or delete anything already in your account
  • Reach your password, email, or payment details

Changed your mind? Cut it off instantly. Revoke access any time in Settings, then AI Assistant. The moment you do, the connection stops working, no win can be written afterward.

Quick answers

Common questions

Which AI assistants work with this?
Any assistant that supports custom connectors (the open MCP standard). You add Bragsheet as a connector and approve it, most assistants sign you in through Bragsheet, with no key to copy.
Is my Bragsheet data sent to the AI?
No. The connector is write-only, your assistant can hand a new win to Bragsheet, but it can't pull anything back out. Your existing wins, profile, and account details are never exposed.
What if a connection is compromised?
Revoke access in Settings, then AI Assistant, it's rejected immediately, and even a leaked connection can only ever add wins, never read or delete. If you used a manual bsk_ key for an older client, treat it like a password and don't paste it into public places.
It won't connect in ChatGPT, what's wrong?
Almost always a plan limit, not a bug. Free ChatGPT accounts can't add custom connectors at all, you need Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise, or Edu with Developer mode on. Claude works on any plan, including Free, so that's the quickest way to get unblocked.
Does it cost anything?
Connecting an assistant is free. Some limits may apply to how many wins can be logged in a short window to keep things fair, but everyday use won't hit them.
